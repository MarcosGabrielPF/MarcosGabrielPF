About Me

Hi! I'm Marcos Gabriel, a developer and technology enthusiast. I enjoy exploring the limits of programming, understanding how things work under the hood, and turning ideas into real projects.

My focus is on experimenting, learning, and building. I work with different programming languages and technologies, with a special interest in software development, operating systems, low-level programming, and creating my own tools. I believe programming goes far beyond writing code: it's about understanding problems and finding interesting ways to solve them.

In my development environment, Linux is home. I use Zorin OS and enjoy customizing my desktop, exploring command-line tools, and experimenting with new technologies. I'm always learning, developing projects, and occasionally building solutions that could probably be much simpler.

💡 My philosophy: understand how it works, discover how to improve it, and, if necessary, build it from scratch.
